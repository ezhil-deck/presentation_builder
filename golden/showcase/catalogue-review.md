# Two shifts change how teams approve spend

```items
- heading: Mobile first
  body: Managers now approve most requests from a phone notification.
- heading: Policy as rules
  body: Spending limits run as checks before any money leaves.
```

- Move every approval out of email
- Set limits per team and per supplier
- Route large requests to two approvers
- Review exceptions once a week


None of the three changes needed a new team or a new system. Each one moved a step that used to happen at month-end into the day the money was spent, so the close became a review instead of a hunt for paperwork.

# What each module does

```items
- heading: Purchases
  body: Requests are raised in one form, checked against the policy and routed to the right approver; once approved, the order goes to the supplier with its budget line attached.
- heading: Expenses
  body: Employees photograph a receipt and the platform reads the amount, date and merchant, matches it to the card line and files it under the right cost center.
- heading: Invoices
  body: Supplier invoices are captured from a shared inbox, matched to their purchase order and paid on the due date, with any price difference flagged for review.
- heading: Cards
  body: Every employee card carries its own limits per merchant and per month; finance can freeze a card in one click and sees each card line the next morning.
```

# The four phases of a rollout

```process
- heading: Connect
  body: Finance, HR and banking systems are linked in a single afternoon, and users, cost centers and suppliers come in from the files the customer already keeps.
- heading: Configure
  body: The customer's spending policy becomes approval rules in one working session, with limits per team, per supplier and per amount, reviewed by finance before launch.
- heading: Pilot
  body: One department runs every real request through the platform for two weeks, while the onboarding lead adjusts the rules wherever approvals stall or bounce back.
- heading: Go live
  body: Every other team moves over in a single week, email approvals are switched off, and the first month closes on the platform with the onboarding lead on hand.
```

# Two measures the board follows every quarter

```kpis
- value: 118%
  heading: Net revenue retention
  body: Revenue kept and grown from last year's customers.
- value: 5.4%
  heading: Gross revenue churn per year
  body: Revenue lost from customers who left or moved down.
```

- Retention is reported per segment, so a weak quarter in one group cannot hide behind a strong one elsewhere.
- Churn counts downgrades as well as cancellations, which makes the number stricter than the usual definition.
- Both measures are reviewed with the board every quarter, alongside the plan agreed at the start of the year.
